Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: a neighborhood deserves a welcoming place for fresh bread and shared treats.

Our small team prepares manageable batches so we can give each loaf and pastry the time it needs.

Thoughtful ingredients

We value reliable ingredients and work toward relationships with nearby suppliers when seasonal availability allows.

Ask us about ingredients before placing a preorder. We cannot guarantee that products are free from allergen cross contact.
